Epiphrenic diverticula
are pulsion-type false diverticula arising from the distal 10cm of the
esophagus
. They are associated with underlying motility disorders generating elevated intraluminal pressure.
Barium swallow
complements endoscopy by characterizing size, neck width, and location, and by providing dynamic motility assessment. Management ranges from conservative in asymptomatic cases to surgical diverticulectomy with esophageal myotomy in symptomatic cases.
