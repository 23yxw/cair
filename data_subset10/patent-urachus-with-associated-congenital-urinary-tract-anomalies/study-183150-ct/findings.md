Post-micturition CT demonstrates a patent urachal tract arising from the anterosuperior aspect of the urinary bladder and extending anteriorly towards the umbilicus, with contrast leakage towards the umbilicus, confirming persistent vesicoumbilical communication. Marked pubic symphysis diastasis , with the urinary bladder positioned between the separated pubic bones. Horseshoe configuration of the kidneys is present with fusion of the lower poles. Partial duplication of the left collecting system is also seen, with separate proximal ureteric channels and distal fusion. Bilateral inguinal hernias containing bowel loops are present without obstruction or incarceration
