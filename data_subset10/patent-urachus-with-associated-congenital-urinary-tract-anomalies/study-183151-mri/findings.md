MRI pelvis demonstrates a tubular fluid signal intensity tract arising from the anterosuperior aspect of the urinary bladder and extending anteriorly towards the umbilicus, corresponding to the patent urachal tract demonstrated on CT. Marked pubic symphysis diastasis is again seen with abnormal anterior positioning of the urinary bladder between the separated pubic bones. The pelvic configuration, in association with the clinical finding of epispadias and dorsal chordee, is suggestive of an underlying genitourinary epispadias spectrum anomaly.
