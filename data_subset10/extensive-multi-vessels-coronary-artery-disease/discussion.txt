A case of extensive
coronary artery disease
with high-grade stenosis in multiple coronary vessels  and extensive plaque burden (calcium score >1500).
The finding correlates most closely with a CAD-RADS 4b/P4 score.
Given the patient's symptoms and the CT findings, the patient  requires an invasive coronary angiogram with intervention.
